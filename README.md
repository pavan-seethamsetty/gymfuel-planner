# GymFuel Planner: AI Meal & Grocery Chatbot

**Course:** AIML-500 AI Lab, Indiana Wesleyan University  
**Built with:** Mizou (chatbot builder), generative AI / LLMs, prompt engineering, design thinking  
**Live chatbot:** [Try GymFuel Planner](https://mizou.com/preview-bot?ID=167371)

## Problem

Planning meals that match a fitness goal takes hours of research. Generic recipe apps don't connect the goal, the meal plan, and the shopping list.

## What it does

GymFuel Planner is a conversational chatbot for fitness-focused users.

1. It asks about your fitness goal, dietary preferences, and available cooking time.
2. It generates a personalized meal plan with a macro breakdown.
3. It turns the plan into an organized grocery list.

It is free to use and needs no sign-up.

## Process

1. **Empathize:** Interviewed potential users and defined personas and pain points.
2. **Define and design:** Designed the conversation flow and wrote the system prompts.
3. **Prototype:** Built the bot on the Mizou platform.
4. **Iterate:** Refined prompts until meal plans and grocery lists came out consistently.
5. **Test:** Tested with users and refined based on feedback.

## Disclaimer

GymFuel Planner gives general meal-planning suggestions. It is not medical or dietary advice.
